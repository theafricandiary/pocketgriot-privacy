Pocket Griot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 11 November 2026

Pocket Griot collects only what is necessary to run the app. To play, you will be asked to create a username. This is the only personal information we collect.

1. Who We Are

Pocket Griot is an independently developed quiz app created to bring Africa and its diaspora's contributions to world history out of the forgotten chapters by making learning fun again.

2. Data We Collect

To play Pocket Griot, you are required to create a username. This username is used solely to display your score and country on the leaderboard. All other quiz progress and preferences are stored locally on your device and we have no access to them.

3. Analytics and Tracking

Pocket Griot does not use advertising networks or tracking technologies. We use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage patterns such as which quiz categories are most played. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Pocket Griot offers optional paid subscriptions. All payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive, store, or process your payment card details.

5. Firebase

We use Google Firebase to store leaderboard data and to monitor app stability through Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase is a Google service and is subject to Google's privacy policy. We do not use Firebase advertising features.

6. Children's Privacy

Pocket Griot is suitable for users of all ages. The only data we store on our servers is a username chosen by the user. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service we use is Google Firebase for leaderboard storage and app stability monitoring. We do not integrate with social networks, advertising platforms, or any other third-party services.

8. Data Security

Leaderboard data stored on Firebase is protected by Google's security infrastructure. Because we collect only a display name and score with no email or identifying information, the risk to users is minimal.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and applicable data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. If you would like your display name and leaderboard scores removed, contact us at the address below and we will delete them within 30 days. You can also clear locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data through your device settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy we will update the effective date above and notify users through the app where required by law.
